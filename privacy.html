# Cat's Clicker Corp. — Privacy Policy Effective Date: 21/Out/2026 ## 1. Introduction This Privacy Policy explains how Cat's Clicker Corp. ("the Game") handles information when you play the Game, use Discord Rich Presence, or participate in cooperative multiplayer sessions. Cat's Clicker Corp. is an independent game. Discord and NodeTunnel are separate third-party services and are not operated by the Game's developer. ## 2. Information Processed by the Game ### A. Local Gameplay Data The Game may process gameplay information needed to operate its features, including progress, rewards, and cooperative session state. The Game also uses a local save file to store a gameplay-related counter associated with rewards earned by a guest player in cooperative sessions. This information is stored locally by the Game. The developer does not operate a dedicated game-progress database or account server. Local files may remain on your device until they are deleted by the Game, by you, or through other applicable device or storage-management processes. ### B. Discord Rich Presence The Game uses the open-source `discord-rich-presence-godot` plugin by SlayHorizon to communicate with the Discord desktop client through Discord Rich Presence. When this feature is active, the Game sends configured activity information to Discord. This may include: - The Game's name. - Your current in-game activity or state. - Relevant multiplayer session or party information configured by the Game. The integration is intended to display game activity. The Game does not intentionally use this integration to read your private Discord messages, access your password, or retrieve your friends list. Discord processes activity information under its own privacy policy. Whether other Discord users can see your activity depends on Discord's functionality and your account settings. Discord Privacy Policy: https://discord.com/privacy ### C. Cooperative Multiplayer and NodeTunnel The Game uses NodeTunnel for relay-based cooperative multiplayer connections. During a multiplayer session, gameplay and session information may be transmitted between the host and guest through the networking connection. This may include gameplay actions, synchronized game state, cursor positions, reward information, and session-related information needed to operate cooperative features. The Game does not operate its own dedicated multiplayer account or gameplay database. NodeTunnel is an independent service. Its processing of connection metadata, network information, logs, and other technical information depends on its implementation and policies. The developer does not claim that NodeTunnel collects no such information or that relay data is never logged or retained. For details about NodeTunnel's processing practices, consult its official website and any applicable privacy documentation: https://nodetunnel.io/ ## 3. How Information Is Used Information processed by the Game is used as necessary to: - Run gameplay and maintain local progress. - Display the Game's activity through Discord Rich Presence. - Establish and maintain cooperative multiplayer sessions. - Synchronize gameplay actions and session state between players. - Support the functionality and security of the Game. The Game does not intentionally use these features to build a separate advertising profile of players. ## 4. Information Sharing Information is transmitted to Discord when Rich Presence is used and to NodeTunnel or the relevant networking infrastructure when cooperative multiplayer is used. Gameplay information may also be transmitted between the host and guest as required for cooperative gameplay. The developer does not operate a dedicated game-progress database. This does not mean that third-party services involved in connectivity or activity display do not process technical information. Third-party services operate independently and are responsible for describing their own data-processing practices. ## 5. Storage and Retention Gameplay-related information may be stored locally on your device, including the local guest-reward counter. The Game's developer does not operate a dedicated server for storing player accounts or persistent multiplayer progress. The developer cannot specify NodeTunnel's exact retention periods for connection metadata or relay-related logs without confirmation from the provider. Please consult NodeTunnel's applicable documentation for information about its retention practices. Discord's handling and retention of activity information are governed by Discord's own policies. ## 6. Your Choices and Privacy Requests You can use Discord's available privacy settings to control the visibility of your activity, where supported. You can remove local Game data by deleting the relevant local files, although doing so may affect locally stored progress or rewards. For questions about personal information or requests concerning information handled by the Game's developer, contact the developer using the details below. Requests concerning information independently processed by Discord or NodeTunnel may need to be directed to those providers. ## 7. Security The developer takes reasonable steps to maintain the Game's functionality and protect information under the developer's control. However, no software or network transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. ## 8. Children's Privacy The Game is not intended to circumvent age restrictions imposed by applicable law or third-party services. If you believe that personal information has been provided to the developer inappropriately, please contact the developer so the matter can be reviewed. ## 9.
